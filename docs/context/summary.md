# dumphere

dumphere is an AI-native artifact-sharing tool for teams. Publish HTML or Markdown
from an AI agent session, share a stable link, and discuss or update the artifact.

## Primary Features

- **Publishing:** Publish HTML and Markdown artifacts through MCP or REST.
- **Sharing:** Keep artifacts internal or enable public links for 30 days.
- **Viewing and downloads:** View artifacts in the browser and download their original source.
- **Personal artifacts:** Browse your other published artifacts, newest first.
- **Comments:** Create document-level or element-attached threads, reply, and navigate
  between comments and their locations. Anonymous commenting is supported.
- **Updates:** Replace or edit artifact content without changing its link.
- **Live preview:** Automatically refresh open artifacts as their content changes.
- **Agent access:** Let authorized AI agents read, publish, and maintain artifacts.
